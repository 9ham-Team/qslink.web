Privacy Notice

Version and effective date: 2026-11-12

Collection and purposes

This Site processes registration emails, usernames, display names, callsigns, salted password hashes, login sessions, submitted card images and text, likes, exchange requests, messages, and recipient information for account verification, login, security, card display, and physical card exchanges. Verification codes are sent through the operator's email provider, which receives the email address and message content needed for delivery. This Site records the Terms version, confirmation time, and account confirming card authorization as evidence, and does not additionally collect IP addresses for this purpose.

Contest voting

When you vote in a contest, This Site records your voting account, selected entry, and voting date and time to count votes and enforce per-account rules. Browser local storage keeps a random voting identifier. The server stores only its HMAC digest to enforce a shared five-vote quota across accounts using the same browser and to help authorized administrators investigate unusual voting. No browser hardware diagnostics are collected and accounts are not automatically banned. The server also limits high-frequency voting requests by source IP, keeping rate-limit data briefly in process memory. Digests and vote records may be linked to accounts and are not anonymous; they are not used for advertising tracking or unrelated marketing.

Browser identifier digests are cleared 90 days after contest voting ends; vote records are retained for vote counting. When an account is closed, retained vote records are de-identified by removing information directly linking them to the account, without canceling votes already cast. Public pages show entry vote counts, not voters or identifier digests. Authorized administrators may view and export vote records within their permissions solely for vote counting, preventing duplicate voting, and manual investigation of unusual voting.

Visibility

Usernames, display names, callsigns, descriptions, and approved cards may be shown publicly. Card images are served at public URLs and visitors who know a link may access them directly; images are unsuitable for private information. Registration emails and dedicated address fields are not displayed in public card profiles.

An exchange request includes the name, address, and message you submit. After the other party agrees, both parties can view necessary recipient details through the exchange process. Administrators with appropriate permissions may view profiles, exchange records, and addresses for review, dispute resolution, and maintenance. Addresses are therefore not technically visible only to the two parties. Submit only information necessary for the exchange.

Storage and service providers

Business data is stored in the PostgreSQL database configured for This Site, and uploaded files are kept in its configured storage. Browser local storage keeps a login token to maintain your login state and saves your interface language preference and random voting browser identifier. Servers, reverse proxies, and email services may keep logs necessary for security and delivery records. Before opening registration, the actual operator should disclose its identity, contact channels, hosting locations, service providers, backup retention period, and any applicable cross-border processing arrangements through a site announcement, and obtain any separate consent required by law. This Notice does not replace those requirements.

Retention, access, and deletion

The operator should retain data only as long as needed to provide the service, protect security, and fulfill legal obligations. View and correct profile information, delete cards in “My Cards,” and withdraw requests according to their status on the exchange page. For account closure, original image removal, data access, or other rights requests, contact the operator named in the site announcement.

Deleting a card does not immediately remove original images from disk or backups, and the original link may remain available. The operator should promptly handle removal requests and explain when backups will expire and be deleted. Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal. Data retained for legal obligations must be segregated for that purpose, and deleted information must not become public again through backup restoration.

Limits on use and changes

The software license does not authorize use of personal information. The content license does not give This Site the right to sell personal information or use it for unrelated marketing. Third parties may save public images; remove sensitive information before uploading. Material changes to processing purposes, public scope, or providers must be disclosed and necessary consent obtained as required by law.
